## Analytics paragraphs

On the main Kat Chapman RN website, Google Analytics, HubSpot analytics, and a configured Meta pixel measure visits only when Cloudflare identifies the visitor's country as the United States. Measurement is limited to the home, about, gallery, privacy, and terms pages. These tools stay off for other or unknown countries. A Global Privacy Control signal or a saved opt-out keeps them off. There is no consent popup. A short opt-out notice appears while measurement is on. The United States limit is a conservative choice, not a guarantee that this setup meets every privacy law.

Contact forms work separately. The name, email, phone number, and message you submit go to HubSpot even if measurement is off. Treatment, booking, consult, and form pages do not load HubSpot analytics.

On the main Kat Chapman RN website, Google Analytics, HubSpot analytics, and Meta stay off on all other pages, including treatment, booking, consult, and form pages. They also stay off when the previous page URL or the current URL's query contains sensitive information. On measured pages, Google Analytics and Meta can receive page URLs, titles, referrers, technical and cookie identifiers, and permitted campaign identifiers. We do not add your name, email, phone number, message, or appointment time to Google or Meta event data. Google ad storage and advertising signals stay off.

Meta loads only for a measured United States visit with a configured pixel ID. Automatic matching stays off. This site sends only a page-view event to Meta, not form, booking, or procedure events.

HubSpot analytics and Google Analytics measure page views on the listed pages. They can also measure clicks to call, text, or open an external scheduler. A Book click is not a completed booking. This site does not send booking events to Meta.

The blog uses HubSpot analytics. Google Analytics and Meta are off on the blog.
