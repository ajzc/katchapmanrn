If you look in the mirror with a straight face two weeks later and wonder what changed, that's normal. Smile, and you'll see it.

Flip, gummy smile, or volume?

These get mixed up. A lip flip is about the top lip curling in. A gummy smile, where a lot of gum shows when you smile, is a different concern that I also treat with neurotoxin, and I'll tell you which one you're seeing. Wanting more lip, even at rest, is a volume question, and that's HA filler for lip volume. At the consult I'll tell you which one fits what you're seeing.

Cost

A lip flip is 4 to 6 units on my pricing page. At $13 per unit, that's $52 to $78. You pay for the units used, and I confirm the count at your free consult. Other neurotoxin areas the same day are also $13 per unit; see Botox in Cornelius.

Does it hurt?

Expect a few quick, sharp pinches. The lip is more sensitive than other areas, but the injections take a couple of minutes.

Downtime

Most people go straight back to their day. A small bump, some redness, or a bruise where the needle went in can show for a short while.

How long it lasts

You'll see it build over several days and settle by about two weeks. MedlinePlus says cosmetic Botox for frown lines, forehead lines, and crow's feet may be repeated every 3 to 4 months, depending on how long the effect lasts. That guidance covers those areas, not this one, so I won't promise a number.

Labeling, in plain words

The FDA prescribing information for Botox Cosmetic lists the approved cosmetic areas: frown lines, crow's feet, forehead lines, and neck (platysma) bands. The upper lip isn't on that list, so this is an off-label use. Off-label use is common in aesthetics, and I'd rather tell you up front than have you find out later.

Your first visit

I ask you to smile, talk, and relax your face while I watch the upper lip. If a flip will make a visible difference, I tell you the units and the price, and we can usually do it that visit. If it won't, I'll say so. At your two-week follow-up I can add a unit or two at no charge.

Safety

Botox and Dysport are prescription drugs. MedlinePlus summarizes the patient information for onabotulinumtoxinA (Botox) and abobotulinumtoxinA (Dysport). Hold off and tell me at your consult if:

  • You're pregnant, planning to be, or breastfeeding.
  • You have a condition that affects muscles or nerves, such as myasthenia gravis, ALS, or Lambert-Eaton syndrome, or you've had trouble swallowing or breathing.
  • You've had a side effect from any botulinum toxin product before.
  • You have a big event in the next two weeks.

The FDA prescribing information for Botox Cosmetic lists side effects such as eyelid or brow drooping and headache.
