Pain, downtime, and a day on the water

Most people call it a quick pinch, and studio visits usually run 20 to 30 minutes. There's no real downtime. A small bump, redness, or a bruise at an injection site is possible. I go over aftercare before you leave, so ask then if you've got plans on the lake that afternoon.

How long results last

Expect a change in the first few days and a settled result at about two weeks. MedlinePlus notes that cosmetic Botox for frown lines, forehead lines, and crow's feet may be repeated every 3 to 4 months, depending on how long the effect lasts. I won't promise a number for your face. If you're planning for summer photos, book at least two weeks ahead.

Your first visit

We talk first. I watch your face move and we agree on a plan. I dose on the light side and check the result at your included two-week follow-up, adjusting at no charge if needed.

Safety

Botox is a prescription drug. Before I inject anything, tell me if you're pregnant, planning to be, or breastfeeding, if you have a nerve or muscle condition, if there's swelling or infection where we'd inject, or if you've reacted to a botulinum toxin product before. The full list, with sources, is on my Botox in Huntersville page.

Check my license on the North Carolina Board of Nursing site. The CDC's page on botulinum toxin injections is worth reading before you book anyone. My full menu is on the services page.
