Medical director supervision

My medical director has 23 years of practice and about 12 years in aesthetics. You can look up my license on the North Carolina Board of Nursing site.

What it is

PRP stands for platelet-rich plasma. PRP BioFiller starts with your own blood, which is prepared into a gel and used for volume. That's what my menu means by a natural volumizing option.

What I can and can't tell you

I can tell you what it is and how I'd use it on your face. What I won't do is quote a duration. There's no FDA or MedlinePlus duration guidance for PRP BioFiller that I can cite, so I won't put a number on this page.

It isn't HA, and it isn't reversible

Hyaluronidase, the enzyme that dissolves HA filler, works on HA. PRP BioFiller isn't HA, so it can't be dissolved that way. If being able to undo a result matters to you, HA filler is the reversible choice.

Cost

PRP BioFiller is priced per session and quoted at consult on my pricing page. You get a specific number at your free consult.

Does it hurt?

It starts with a small blood draw, then the injections, which most people describe as pressure and pinches. We talk through numbing at your consult.

Downtime

Plan on some swelling and a possible bruise where it's injected. Most people go back to their day.

How long it lasts

I'll be straight: I don't have a sourced number for you, so I won't promise one. We'll look at how your result holds over time.

Your first visit

A free consult first. I'll explain how the visit works, look at the area you want to change, and tell you whether PRP BioFiller, HA filler, or a biostimulator fits better.

Is it safe? When to wait

At the consult I'll go over your health history to see if it's right for you. Get care right away for unusual pain, any change in your vision, skin near the injection that turns white, gray, or blue, or any sign of a stroke. After hours, call or text (704) 584-9785. In an emergency, call 911.
