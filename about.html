About Kat

Kat Chapman, BSN, RN in Huntersville, NC

The Nurse First

Medicine isn’t a backstory. It’s the whole point.

Seven years as a Registered Nurse: emergency rooms, hospital floors, and travel contracts across six states. As a hospital RN, I’ve given thousands of injections, so a steady, precise hand is second nature. Aesthetics isn’t a pivot away from medicine for me. It’s medicine applied to helping you feel like yourself.

That background shapes how I work. I take a health history seriously, I ask about medications and past reactions before anything is injected, and I’ll tell you when something isn’t safe for you right now.

Where I work

Canvas & Glow on Sam Furr Road.

I see patients at Canvas & Glow on Sam Furr Road in Huntersville, where I work as the RN injector. Skin and hair services are available at the same location, and you can ask about them at your consult.

Every treatment is done under the supervision of a medical director. My medical director has 23 years of practice and about 12 years in aesthetics. In North Carolina, a registered nurse injects under physician-directed protocols, and you can look up my license on the North Carolina Board of Nursing site.

I live in Birkdale, so this is my neighborhood too. Patients come from Huntersville, Cornelius, Davidson, and around Lake Norman.

The Mission & the Journey

New at this, and not hiding it.

I’m new to aesthetic injecting, and I’m not hiding that. I’m documenting it. Every course, every rep, every result, in public, because you deserve to see exactly who’s treating you and how seriously I take getting great.

My goal is to become one of the most trusted injectors on Lake Norman. Trust comes from showing the work, starting conservatively, and seeing every patient again at two weeks.

How I treat

You get my full attention from consultation to follow-up, a plan built for your face instead of a sales script, and my honest opinion, including when the honest opinion is “you don’t need this.”

  • Every plan starts with a free consultation.
  • I dose on the light side and add at two weeks if you want more.
  • All neurotoxins are $13 per unit. Filler is quoted at consult. Details are on the pricing page.
  • Every treatment includes a complimentary two-week follow-up, with adjustments at no charge.

What I do

Neurotoxins and hyaluronic acid filler: Botox, lip filler, cheek filler, jawline filler, under-eye filler, and facial balancing. I also offer mobile visits for small groups. The full list is on injectables in Huntersville, and common questions are answered on the FAQ.
