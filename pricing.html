How filler is priced

Filler prices in Huntersville, NC depend on three things: how many areas we treat, how much product each area needs, and which filler fits the job. That’s why I quote filler at your free consult instead of posting a per-syringe number that might not match your face.

  • Product. I use Juvéderm. Different HA fillers do different jobs, and I pick the right one for each area at your consult.
  • Amount. Lips are often a small amount. Cheeks, jawline, or a full facial balancing plan usually take more.
  • Biostimulators. Sculptra and PRP BioFiller are priced per session, and Radiesse per syringe or session.

You get a specific estimate before anything is booked, and nothing is injected until you’ve said yes to the number.

How long filler lasts, and why it matters for price

A filler price is easier to judge when you know how long the result might last. That depends on the product, the area, and your body, so I give you a realistic range at your consult rather than a promise here. Hyaluronic acid fillers are reversible, which is part of why I start conservatively.

Plan on a few days of swelling and the chance of a bruise. The FDA lists swelling and bruising among the most common dermal filler side effects and says many clear up within days to weeks. The FDA’s dermal filler dos and don'ts are worth a read before you book with anyone.

What’s included in the price

Every price covers a free consultation, a plan built for your face, the treatment itself done by me under the supervision of a medical director, and a complimentary two-week follow-up. If something needs adjusting at two weeks, I do it at no charge.

How much is filler?

Filler is priced per syringe and quoted at your free consult, because the amount and product depend on your face and the areas you want treated. You get a specific estimate before anything is booked.

How much is Botox?

All neurotoxins, including Botox, Dysport, Xeomin, Jeuveau, and Letybo, are $13 per unit. The table on this page shows typical units by area, and the Botox cost page walks through a full visit.

Why per unit instead of per area?

Per-unit pricing is more honest. Flat per-area pricing often means paying for units you don’t need. Kat only charges for what she actually uses. All neurotoxins are $13/unit.
