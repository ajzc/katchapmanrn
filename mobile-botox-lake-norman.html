Does it hurt, and how long does it last?

Most people call Botox a quick pinch. Results show over the first few days and settle at about two weeks. MedlinePlus notes that cosmetic Botox for frown lines, forehead lines, and crow's feet may be repeated every 3 to 4 months, depending on how long the effect lasts. I won't promise a number for your face.

There's no real downtime. A little redness or a small bump where the needle went in is common for a short while, and a bruise is possible. I go over aftercare with each guest.

Your follow-up

Every treatment includes a complimentary two-week follow-up. That's when I check the settled result and adjust at no charge if something needs it. Book mobile visits at least two weeks before a wedding or photos, so the result has time to settle.

Who should wait

Botox and Dysport are prescription drugs. MedlinePlus summarizes the patient information for onabotulinumtoxinA (Botox) and abobotulinumtoxinA (Dysport). Tell me at your private consult, before anything is injected, if any of these apply:

  • You're pregnant, planning to be, or breastfeeding.
  • You have a condition that affects muscles or nerves, such as myasthenia gravis, ALS, or Lambert-Eaton syndrome, or you've had trouble swallowing or breathing.
  • There's swelling or any sign of infection where we'd inject.
  • You've had a side effect from any botulinum toxin product before.

If one applies, we talk it through privately before anything happens. Nobody gets treated just to keep the evening on schedule.
