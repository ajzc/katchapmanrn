Why I price per unit

A unit is a measured dose of the neurotoxin. Pricing per unit means you pay for what I actually use. A flat price per area charges the same whether your forehead takes 10 units or 20, so some people pay for units they never get, and others get fewer units than they need. Per-unit pricing keeps the number tied to your face.

It also means first-timers usually spend less than they feared. I dose on the light side, and adding a few units at your two-week follow-up is easy.

Does Dysport cost more than Botox?

No. Botox, Dysport, Xeomin, Jeuveau, and Letybo are all $13 per unit. The products are dosed differently, so unit counts can vary by product and area. I recommend whichever one fits your goals and quote it exactly at your visit.

  • A free consultation to map your goals and give you an exact quote.
  • A plan built for your face.
  • Your treatment done under the supervision of a medical director.
  • A complimentary two-week follow-up, with adjustments at no charge if you want a tweak. That's the Natural Results Guarantee.

The follow-up matters for your budget. Most first-timers worry about looking frozen or wasting money. Coming back at two weeks lets us fix either one without paying again.

Paying for it

Ask about payment methods at your visit. Financing through Cherry and CareCredit is available for treatment plans. Ask at your consult about manufacturer rewards programs, such as Allē and Aspire, where they apply to the product used.

How often you'll pay

Botox wears off gradually, so cost is a repeat expense. MedlinePlus notes that cosmetic Botox for frown lines, forehead lines, and crow's feet may be repeated every 3 to 4 months, depending on how long the effect lasts. Your timing may be different, and we'll talk about it at your follow-up.

Is cheap Botox worth the risk?

A low price should come with two questions: who is injecting, and where did the product come from? The CDC has tracked people who were hurt after injections with counterfeit or unlabeled product bought online, or by someone without a valid healthcare license. Its page on botulinum toxin injections covers what to watch for. You can look up my license on the North Carolina Board of Nursing site, and the FDA prescribing information for Botox Cosmetic is public if you want to read it.

What I can't tell you from a webpage

I can't tell you your exact unit count without watching your face move. If your plan includes filler, filler is priced per syringe and quoted at your consult, and I cover that on my facial balancing page. For everything else, see the full services menu.
