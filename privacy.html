Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This page explains what information katchapmanrn.com collects and how it is used. The site belongs to Kat Chapman, BSN, RN.

Analytics and tracking

The site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are viewed. It also loads the HubSpot tracking code, which sets cookies (including the hubspotutk cookie) to measure visits and connect form or booking activity to a visitor session. These tools may collect information such as your browser type, device, pages visited, and approximate location based on your IP address.

Booking

Appointments are booked through HubSpot Meetings, which opens on a HubSpot page. The details you enter there, such as your name and contact information, are collected through HubSpot so your appointment can be scheduled and confirmed.

Phone and text

If you call or text (704) 584-9785, your phone number and anything you send are used to respond to you and help with your appointment.

What the site does not collect

Treatment and health records are not collected through this website. Those are handled at your in-person visit.

Selling personal information

Your personal information is not sold.

Questions

Questions about this policy? Call or text (704) 584-9785.
